Our Background

Tendrich Innovations SMC LTD is a privately owned company opened and established in 2023, dealing in the installation of CCTV Camera, Automatic Gate System, Solar System, Electric Fence, Razor Wire, Carports, Electrical Wiring, Motion Sensor Systems, Car Shades, Fire Fighting Systems and Access Control Systems.

Our Vision

To become one of the leading innovations in the construction, power and security industry in East Africa.

Our Mission

To supply and install quality systems that reflect the value of money that clients invest, hence improving security and better living.
